HSK 1 FAQ

How long does the HSK 1 exam take?

The test lasts 40 minutes: 20 minutes listening and 20 minutes reading (including time to fill in personal info).

Do I need to know Chinese before starting?

No prior knowledge is required. HSK 1 assumes you are starting from zero and focuses on pronunciation basics and essential vocabulary.

What should I do the week before the exam?

Prioritize tone practice, vocabulary review, and listening drills. Use our analyzer to check HSK 1 words in any text you encounter.
