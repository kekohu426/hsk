HSK 3 FAQ

How long does the HSK 3 exam take?

The test lasts 85 minutes: 35 minutes listening, 30 minutes reading, and 20 minutes writing (including time to fill in personal info).

Do I need to finish HSK 1 and 2 first?

Not officially, but we recommend completing the HSK 2 modules in ChineseMaster or passing a placement quiz before starting.

What should I focus on the week before the exam?

Prioritize mock exams, listening drills, and reviewing writing patterns. Use our analyzer to check weak vocabulary areas.
