HSK 2 FAQ

How long does the HSK 2 exam take?

The test lasts 55 minutes: 25 minutes listening and 30 minutes reading (including time to fill in personal info).

Should I retake HSK 1 before HSK 2?

Only if you struggle with tones or the basics. Otherwise, start HSK 2 and review HSK 1 vocabulary during the first two weeks.

What should I focus on the week before the exam?

Prioritize listening drills, dialogues, and reviewing comparison sentences. Use our analyzer to double-check frequent words.
