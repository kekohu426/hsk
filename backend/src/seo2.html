朋友

péng you (peng2 you3)

friend

HSK2

名词 (noun)

★★★★☆ HSK2高频词汇

核心含义

中文解释

指彼此之间有良好关系、相互信任、能互相帮助的人，是日常生活中常见的社交关系对象，可用于描述同性或异性之间的友好联结

English Definition

A person with whom one has a good relationship, mutual trust, and can help each other; a common social relationship in daily life, which can describe friendly connections between same-sex or opposite-sex individuals

常见搭配

好朋友

hǎo péng you (hao3 peng2 you3)

good friend

看朋友

kàn péng you (kan4 peng2 you3)

visit a friend

朋友的家

péng you de jiā (peng2 you3 de jia1)

friend's home

详细说明

"朋友"是表示人际关系的常用名词，可用于日常交流、描述生活场景。在汉语中，"朋友"的使用范围较广，既可以指关系亲密的伙伴，也可以指普通的友好之人。

使用时可直接搭配数量词，如"一个朋友""两个朋友"。

"朋友" (friend) is a common noun used to express interpersonal relationships. When using it, you can directly match it with numerals, such as "一个朋友" (one friend) and "两个朋友" (two friends).

易错点

❌ 错误

我有很多朋友们。

Wǒ yǒu hěn duō péng you men.

✅ 正确

我有很多朋友。

Wǒ yǒu hěn duō péng you.

Explanation: "朋友" (friend) itself is a pluralizable noun in Chinese. When preceded by quantifiers like "很多" (many), adding the plural suffix "们" is redundant.

真题例句

我和朋友去公园。

Wǒ hé péng you qù gōng yuán.

I go to the park with my friend.

来源: HSK2 听力Part3

她是我的好朋友。

Tā shì wǒ de hǎo péng you.

She is my good friend.

来源: HSK2 阅读Part1

今天我要去看朋友。

Jīn tiān wǒ yào qù kàn péng you.

Today I am going to visit a friend.

来源: HSK2 写作Part1

文化背景

在中国文化中，"朋友"是重要的人际关系概念，讲究"患难与共""互帮互助"。传统上有"益者三友"的说法，指有益的朋友类型。

在日常社交中，人们常用"认识一下，交个朋友"开启新的社交关系，体现了对友好人际关系的重视。

In Chinese culture, "朋友" (friend) is an important concept of interpersonal relationship, emphasizing "sharing weal and woe" and "mutual help". Traditionally, there is the saying "益者三友" (three types of beneficial friends).

In daily social interactions, people often use "认识一下，交个朋友" (let's get to know each other and be friends) to start new social relationships.
